📊 Statistical Validation

A statistical analysis was performed to investigate the relationship between Sales and Profit.

Hypotheses

Null Hypothesis (H₀):

There is no significant relationship between Sales and Profit.

Alternative Hypothesis (H₁):

There is a significant relationship between Sales and Profit.

Statistical Method

Pearson Correlation Analysis

Confidence Level: 95%
Significance Level: α = 0.05

The statistical analysis was used to support the dashboard insights and evidence-based business decisions.
